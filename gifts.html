Gift Registry

Thank you for visiting our Gift Registry page -- please feel no obligation to bring a gift, your presence is gift enough as we are most excited about bringing our communities together. We've been given the advice that some people will want to bring a gift and "you may as well ask for things that you want". It is in that spirit that we have curated a list of items that we anticipate enjoying for years to come. If you choose to purchase an item from the list (or a similar item to one on the list), please indicate on this page that the item has been purchased (we're hoping to avoid the classic we-got-five-toasters situation). Beyond this list, we hope to purchase a home in the near future and are truly grateful for any financial gifts towards this investment.
