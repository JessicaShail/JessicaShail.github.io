J&S Wedding Weekend Celebrations

We invite you to join us for two days of blended traditions to celebrate this milestone in our relationship. Day 1 includes an afternoon Mehndi event. Day 2 includes a mid-afternoon wedding ceremony, followed by a cocktail hour, dinner, and reception. We are looking forward to bringing our communities together and sharing moments with you during this two-day event.
It's common in India for wedding celebrations to extend over seven days!

Hopes

During this weekend we hope that each one of you is able to fully enjoy the experiences that we've planned including connecting with others and eating delicious food. After the weekend has concluded we hope that you remember a joyful experience and a sense of community (and that you liked the food).

Overall: Lean in to colour -- with the specific request that no one dress in all white, or in all black.

Day 1 is less formal. Feel free to wear something more casual (though you're welcome to dress up). For those who would like to have henna applied to their hands/arms, please wear clothing that allows for this (your clothing may brush up against the paste and be stained).

Day 2 is for dressing up a bit. We anticipate that guests will wear the same outfit for both Day 2 events. Brightly coloured saris, suits, dress shirts, pantsuits, dresses...your choice! Guests will be seated for the ceremony and dinner and there will be a dance lesson (some of you already know about Garba, the rest of you will have the opportunity to learn!).
