Exploring Hamilton

Hamilton? more like Hamilfun! We've got the map for you below (it's been updated), and now are adding a few other 'hot tips' for enjoying "The Winnipeg of Ontario":


  • The Vibe Hamilton is a weekly e-newsletter sharing about loads of upcoming events in the city. There's a Recurring Vibe that you can check out for events that happen regularly.
  • Hamilton pizzeria Cowabunga won the best slice of cheese pizza award at the International Pizza Challenge in 2024! In our opinion it's not the best pizza in town but, hey, we're not the judges of this competition.
  • Thank you to Tourism Hamilton for putting together a list of Heated Rivalry filming locations across the city.
  • Choice Indian Restaurant. A famous Ahmedabad restaurant now in Hamilton - some of Shail's favorites are the Bhakri Pizza (spicy!), Hara Bhara Kebab and the Butter Dabeli.

Getting to Hamilton, Ontario

For those of you who are hopping on a plane to celebrate with us, thank you! Toronto Pearson International Airport is the biggest airport nearby. Traveling between the airport and Hamilton can be done by train, bus, shuttle, or car. There is a GO bus (Route 40) from Terminal 1 Pearson International Airport to Hamilton GO Centre—fare details and schedules are available on the GO Transit website.

The John C. Munro Hamilton International Airport is very small and a lot closer to us. Traveling from this airport into the city can be done by bus, shuttle, or car.

Traveling between accommodation and venues is easiest by car. Hamilton does have a public transit system, and we have Uber/taxis as well.
